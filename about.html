About FriendHub

FriendHub is a free website for meeting new people through random video and text chat. You open the site, allow your camera and microphone, press Start, and you are connected to the next available person. When you want a different conversation, press Stop and Start again. There is no email, no password and no download.

Why we built it

Random chat has a reputation for being chaotic and unsafe. FriendHub is an independent project that tries to keep the part people enjoy — spontaneous conversations with people from other countries and cultures — while putting safety and moderation first: adults only, a visible Report button on every call, and bans for people who break the rules.

How it works

  • Matching. When you press Start you join a waiting pool. The site pairs you with another waiting user, optionally narrowed by the country filter you choose.
  • Video and audio. Calls use WebRTC, so video and audio travel directly between the two browsers whenever the network allows. FriendHub does not record calls.
  • Text chat. A text panel runs alongside the call, so you can keep your camera off and just type if you prefer.
  • Community chat. The community room is a group text chat for talking between video calls.

Safety and moderation

  • FriendHub is for people 18 and over. You must confirm your age before you chat.
  • Use the Report button to end a call and send the user's behaviour to our moderation team. A report can include a single captured frame of the other person's video so moderators can review what happened.
  • Users who break the Community Rules are banned.
  • Our Safety & Privacy Guide explains habits that keep random chat low-risk.

How FriendHub is funded

FriendHub is free to use. The site shows advertising through Google AdSense to cover hosting and development costs. Ads are never placed inside the video call itself. See our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy for details.

The blog
