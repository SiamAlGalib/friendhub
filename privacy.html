Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information FriendHub (“we”, “us”) handles when you use friendhub.space, and the choices you have. FriendHub is intended for people aged 18 and over.

1. Information we handle

  • Camera and microphone. Your browser asks for permission before FriendHub can use your camera and microphone. Video and audio are sent to the person you are matched with, directly between browsers where possible. We do not record or store call video or audio.
  • Anonymous account identifier. To run matching and chat we create an anonymous identifier for your session using Firebase Authentication (Google). You do not need to give an email address or password.
  • Matching and presence data. While you are online we store technical data needed to pair users, such as your anonymous identifier, whether you are online or waiting, the display name or identity option you choose, and your optional country filter.
  • Text messages. Text chat messages are delivered through Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud) so that the other person receives them. Do not send information you would not want the other person to see.
  • Reports. If you press Report, we receive the reason you choose, any details you type (up to 500 characters), the identifiers involved, and, where possible, one captured frame of the reported user's video. This is used only for moderation and safety.
  • Network information. Real-time video requires your device to exchange network addresses (including your IP address) with the other participant and with STUN servers operated by Google. Hosting and security providers may also log IP addresses and browser details in ordinary server logs.
  • Local settings. Your browser's local storage keeps small preferences, such as notification settings, on your own device.

2. How we use information

  • To connect you with other users and operate video and text chat.
  • To review reports, enforce our Terms and Community Rules, and ban abusive users.
  • To keep the service secure and fix technical problems.
  • To show advertising that keeps the site free (see below).

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising

FriendHub uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads, and to show personalised ads where permitted. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com and learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. See our Cookie Policy.

4. Service providers

We rely on Google Firebase (authentication and database), Google STUN servers (connection setup), GitHub Pages (hosting), and Google AdSense (advertising). These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

5. Retention

Matching and presence records are temporary and relate to your active session. Reports and ban records are kept for as long as needed to protect users and enforce our rules. We do not keep call video or audio.

6. Children

FriendHub is not for anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using the site, use the Report button or contact us and we will act on it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can deny camera and microphone access in your browser, use text chat only, clear your browser storage, and opt out of personalised ads. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold. To make a request, contact us.

8. Security

We use reputable infrastructure and access controls, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Be careful about what you share with strangers.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

10. Contact
