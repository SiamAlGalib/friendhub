Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how FriendHub uses cookies and similar technologies such as local storage.

What we use

  • Essential storage. Firebase Authentication keeps an anonymous session in your browser so you stay connected to chat. Local storage holds small preferences, such as notification settings. The site cannot work properly without these.
  • Advertising cookies. Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies to show ads, limit how often you see them, and measure performance. These may be used to personalise ads where permitted.

We do not currently run our own analytics or tracking tools on FriendHub.

Your choices

  • Manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential storage, chat may stop working.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

See also our Privacy Policy. Questions? Contact us.
