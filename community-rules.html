Community Rules

Last updated: November 8, 2026

FriendHub works only if people feel safe. These rules apply to video chat, text chat and the community room.

Not allowed

  • Nudity or sexual content. Do not display, request or describe it.
  • Minors. You must be 18 or older. Never involve, target or exploit anyone under 18.
  • Harassment and hate. No abuse, bullying, stalking, or attacks on people for who they are.
  • Violence and threats. No threats, and no content that encourages harming others or yourself.
  • Spam, scams and advertising. No selling, promoting links, asking for money, or phishing.
  • Privacy violations. Do not record, screenshot or share other people's video, audio or messages without consent, and do not share anyone's personal information.
  • Illegal activity of any kind.
  • Evasion and automation. No bots, and no creating new sessions to get around a ban.

What to do if someone breaks the rules

Press Report in the chat panel. This ends the call and sends your report to our moderators. You can always press Stop and leave without explanation.

What happens next

Moderators review reports and can issue permanent bans. Serious violations, such as anything involving minors, may be reported to the relevant authorities.

Appeals

If you believe you were banned in error, contact us with a description of what happened.
