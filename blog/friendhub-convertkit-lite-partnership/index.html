What is ConvertKit Lite?

ConvertKit Lite is a free online toolkit for everyday file jobs. Most tools process your files directly in your browser, so you don't have to upload sensitive documents to a remote server for common tasks. The tools cover students, freelancers, office workers and anyone who handles documents.

Popular ConvertKit Lite tools

  • Merge PDF: combine several PDFs into one document in the order you choose.
  • Split PDF: extract page ranges, split every N pages or pick individual pages, then download the results as a ZIP.
  • Compress PDF: reduce file size for email and online forms.
  • PDF to Word: turn a PDF into an editable document.
  • JSON to Excel: convert JSON data into a spreadsheet.
  • RTF to PDF: create a shareable PDF from an RTF file.
  • MP4 to MP3: pull the audio out of a video.
  • Remove Background: remove image backgrounds in the browser.
  • Face Age Estimator: a fun tool that guesses how old you look.

Why browser-based, privacy-friendly tools matter

Many online converters make you upload your file to a server and wait. That can be slow, and it raises a fair question: who else can see my document? Tools that process files locally in your browser reduce that concern for everyday tasks. It is the same principle that makes FriendHub easy to use: open a page, do the job, and move on without extra accounts or software.

Whatever tool you use, avoid processing highly confidential files on any website unless you trust it and understand how it works.

Practical tip 1: How to shrink a PDF for email or online forms

Many job portals, university systems and government forms limit uploads to a fixed size. A few reliable steps:

  1. Open the Compress PDF tool.
  2. Select your file.
  3. Compress it and check that the text is still readable.
  4. Download the smaller file and upload it to the form.

If a form asks for a specific limit, use the matching tool: compress PDF to 100KB, 200KB, 500KB, 1MB or 3MB.

Practical tip 2: Edit a PDF by converting it to Word

PDFs are great for sharing but awkward to edit. Use PDF to Word to turn a PDF into an editable document. Check the formatting afterward, especially tables and images, and then save or re-export the final version.

Practical tip 3: Stay safe on random video chat

Meeting strangers online can be fun, but a few habits keep it safe:

  • Never share personal details such as your address, school, workplace, phone number or passwords.
  • Keep your background neutral so nothing identifying is visible behind you.
  • Skip the conversation any time you feel uncomfortable. You never owe anyone an explanation.
  • Report inappropriate behavior so the community stays safe.
  • Don't click links or send money to people you've just met.
